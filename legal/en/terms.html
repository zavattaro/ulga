§2. Nature of the service

Ulga is an app with audio recordings in Polish that help you calm down, rest and fall asleep peacefully: the “First 7 days” (“Pierwsze 7 dni”) program, sleep sessions, short pauses during the day and background sounds with a sleep timer. The content is secular — it is neither religious nor esoteric. The app runs in the browser and on the user's device, including without internet (after downloading the recordings).

§3. Important health disclaimer

Ulga is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR) and does not replace the advice of a doctor or psychotherapist.

The app does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, including sleep disorders. If problems with sleep or well-being persist, contact a doctor. Do not listen to the recordings while driving or operating machinery. In a life-threatening emergency, call 112. Mental health crisis support in Poland: 116 123 (adults) or 116 111 (children and young people).
