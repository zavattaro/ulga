Ulga Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23.11.2026 · English translation of the Polish “Polityka prywatności”; in case of discrepancy, the Polish version prevails.

1. Data controller

The data controller within the meaning of the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council) is 70.144.239 ENIO ZAVATTARO FERNANDES (sole proprietorship — MEI, Brazil), email: appolonia.modlitwa@gmail.com. You can write to this address about any privacy matter.

2. Key points

  • Ulga runs on your device, including without internet. You do not create an account.
  • We do not send any of your data to our servers or anyone else's.
  • We do not use analytics, ads or tracking cookies.
  • We do not ask about your well-being, mood or health and do not record them.
  • You can download or delete all app data at any time.

3. What data the app stores

The app stores only the following, exclusively in the storage of your browser/device (localStorage and Cache Storage):

  • consent — whether you accepted the Terms and Privacy Policy, and the date;
  • progress — number and duration of sessions listened to, day streak, completed program days, a short history of recent sessions;
  • settings — theme, volume, selected sound, timer duration, reminder time;
  • license — premium status and license key (if you enter one);
  • downloaded recordings — session audio files saved for offline listening.

This data does not leave the device. We have no access to it. The history of sessions listened to is not health data within the meaning of Article 9 GDPR — the app does not diagnose or measure sleep — but it stays with you only regardless.

4. Legal basis

Storing this information on the device is necessary to provide the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, as regards storage on the device, is strictly necessary for the app to function (Article 399 of the Polish Electronic Communications Law).

5. Recording files and the server

The app and recordings are downloaded from a hosting server like any website. The hosting provider may technically record the IP address and request time in server logs solely to ensure the security and operation of the service (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not combine these logs with any other data and do not use them for analytics.

6. AI-generated narrator voice

The narrator's voice in the recordings was generated by artificial intelligence (Google Gemini, text-to-speech) from texts written by us. None of your data is used to generate the voice — the recordings are produced in advance and are the same for all users.

7. Payments

Premium purchases are handled by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom) as the seller (Merchant of Record). Paddle processes your email address, payment data and tax settlement in accordance with its own privacy policy (paddle.com/legal/privacy). The Paddle payment script loads only after you click the purchase button. After purchase you receive a license key; the key contains only a hash of part of your email address, not the address itself, and is verified solely on your device. After purchase, the app sends only the Paddle transaction number to our license service (Cloudflare Workers, Cloudflare Inc.) to confirm the payment and receive the license key. The service stores no data; your email address is neither passed to the app nor stored. For subscriptions, the app periodically sends the subscription number to extend the license. Like any server, Cloudflare technically receives your device's IP address in order to handle the request.

8. Calendar reminder

The “Add to calendar” (“Dodaj do kalendarza”) feature creates an .ics file on your device, which you add to your calendar yourself. We do not send it anywhere and have no access to your calendar.

9. Your rights (Articles 15–21 GDPR)

  • Access and portability — in Settings use the “Pobierz moje dane (JSON)” (Download my data) button.
  • Rectification — you change settings directly in the app.
  • Erasure — the “Usuń wszystkie dane” (Delete all data) button in Settings permanently deletes the data and downloaded recordings from the device. The same happens when you uninstall the app or clear your browser data.
  • Restriction and objection — you can delete the data or stop using the app.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych) (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland, uodo.gov.pl).

10. Age

The app is intended for persons aged 16 or over. Younger persons may use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We will inform you of material changes in the app. The current version is always available in Settings.
